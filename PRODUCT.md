# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hosts of small recurring dinners (4–8 adults) who need a practical venue decision without another group chat spiral. Participants opt in with private tastes or explicit practical needs.

## Product Purpose

Common Ground helps a known group agree on where to go. It collects private preferences, ranks a shortlist with hard constraints first, supports private vetoes, and exports a tentative plan. It does not book, charge, or message venues.

## Positioning

Taste signals and hard requirements stay private to members. The host sees aggregates and a shared shortlist, not who vetoed or why. Unknown venue facts stay labeled unknown, never treated as passed.

## Constraints

- Adults only; opt-in cultural profiling; skip path required
- No reservation, payment, invite send, or calendar mutation
- Synthetic demo paths must be labeled and never mistaken for live evidence
- Brand name: Common Ground (working name; mark is overlapping lenses)

## Voice

Short product sentences. No em dashes. No lecture tone. Controls name the action.
