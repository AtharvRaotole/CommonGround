# Privacy (public summary)

Full notice: [docs/privacy/notice.md](privacy/notice.md) · in-app: `/privacy`

## Short version

- Taste seeds, private ranks, and veto reasons are not shown to other members or the host.
- Hosts see completion counts and aggregate shortlist cards.
- Approving a plan is not a reservation and does not charge a card.
- Delete-my-inputs removes your preferences and revokes your session for that outing.
- Telemetry is aggregate and redacted — no seed lists or rank matrices.
- Live provider calls (Qloo / optional OpenAI) only occur when configured; provider terms still apply.

## Retention

Outing-related application data is intended to expire on the order of ~30 days after the outing when provider terms allow. Derived planning revisions target shorter TTL. Hackathon previews may be wiped earlier.
