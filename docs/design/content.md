# Content & microcopy (P05)

Voice: calm, direct, host-respecting. No hype metrics. Sentence case. Active verbs.

## Brand lines

| Placement | Copy |
|---|---|
| Landing H1 | Common Ground |
| Landing support | Agree on where the group goes — without another endless thread. |
| Promise (positioning only) | Get your next small-group outing agreed, with less back-and-forth. |
| Mode chip live | Live planning |
| Mode chip example | Synthetic example — not a live recommendation |

## Unknown facts

- "We don't have a dated source for {fact}. Treat this as unverified — not as a yes."
- "Unknown requirements are not passed requirements."
- "Needs your confirmation before this plan can be marked ready."

## Missing Qloo coverage

- "We couldn't match "{query}" to a catalog entry. Pick another, or continue with hard needs only."
- "Taste ranking is unavailable. Showing guided planning from hard needs and the venue list — not a Qloo ranking."
- "Some members skipped cultural seeds ({n}/{total}). We won't claim full-group cultural fit."

## Private veto

- Button: "Doesn't work for me"
- Helper: "Your name and reason stay private. The host only sees that someone objected."
- After: "Removed one venue after a participant objection. {n} alternatives remain."

## Tentative export

- Banner: "Tentative planning brief — not host-approved / not a reservation."
- Always: "Common Ground does not book tables. You still make the reservation and send invites."
- ICS description must include: `Reservation: unconfirmed. Host must book.`

## Provider failure

- Qloo: "Cultural ranking is temporarily unavailable. Retry, or continue in guided mode."
- OpenAI: "Explanations are offline. Rankings and constraints still apply."
- Generic: "Something failed on our side (ref {requestId}). Your inputs are safe; try again."

## Approval ≠ booking (AC03 teaching copy)

Shown on S10 and S11 permanently:

> Approving confirms the group's plan inside Common Ground. It does **not** reserve a table, charge a card, or message the venue.

## Affinity ≠ enjoyment % (AC03 teaching copy)

On any surface that could show scores:

> Ranking signals order options for this slate. They are not a percentage chance you'll enjoy the meal.

## Access expired

- Invite: "This invite link was already used or expired. Ask the host for a new one."
- Session: "Your access ended. Rejoin with a fresh invite if the host still has open slots."

## Validation (examples)

- "Group size must be between 4 and 8."
- "Choose a timezone — we won't guess daylight-saving."
- "Select which artist/film you mean — we never auto-pick lookalikes."

## Deletion

- Participant: "Delete my inputs for this outing"
- Confirm: "This removes your tastes and hard needs from this outing. You can't undo."
- Host: type outing title to delete entire event.
