# Privacy notice — Common Ground preview

**Last updated:** 2026-10-04 · Matches implemented Worker/D1 behavior.

## What we collect

- Outing settings you enter (title, group size, area, time/timezone when provided).
- Optional cultural “seeds” (up to three confirmed catalog entities) if you opt in.
- Hard needs / constraints you submit (budget, access, dietary tags, vetoes).
- Session capability cookies (HttpOnly) after you redeem a one-time invite.
- Optional post-outing feedback (attendance, fit, planning experience) — missing answers stay missing.
- Aggregate product telemetry (started/completed/replan/approval/export counts). Telemetry never stores seed lists, rank matrices, or veto reasons.

## What hosts see

Hosts see completion counts and shortlist cards with aggregate compromise language. Hosts do **not** see another member’s seed list, private rank vector, or veto reason.

## What we do not do

- We do not book tables, send invites, or charge cards.
- We do not promise that providers retain nothing. OpenAI calls use `store:false` when used; provider abuse-monitoring and terms still apply. Qloo receives entity IDs needed for ranking when a live key is configured.
- We do not treat a no-show as a dislike.

## Retention and deletion

- Derived planning revisions expire (default target ≤24h for run output when permitted).
- Outing-related application data is intended to expire on the order of ~30 days after the outing when provider terms allow — a maximum, not an entitlement.
- **Delete my inputs:** removes your preferences, consents, constraints, vetoes, feedback, and revokes your session for that outing. You remain on the roster for coverage counts only.
- Hosts can duplicate outing **settings** for a repeat; private seeds are **not** copied unless members consent again.
