# Disconfirming evidence, channel bias, workaround sufficiency (P02 AC03)

## Strongest disconfirming evidence

1. **Home/chef supper clubs thrive without external venue choice** (Ate with Eight, Lucky Dinner Club, Supper by Sarah). Recurring dinner ≠ recurring restaurant comparison.
2. **Platforms already sell venue+people packaging** (Timeleft ~$19.99/mo US claim; Super Diners $24.99/mo; Dinner weekly; Dinner Table Club ticketed). Indie hosts may want *distribution*, not another planner.
3. **Free/cheap decision tools exist** (DinePoll, GroupPick, Google Forms, Partiful polls). If the job is “stop the group chat,” these may be “good enough.”
4. **Relationship/corkage constraints dominate some circuits** (Manhattan Wine & Dine). Cultural affinity cannot expand a closed restaurant allowlist.
5. **Traveler / stranger tables** (table for one(s), Timeleft) weaken repeat-compromise and private-veto narratives that assume known communities.
6. **No live host statements of SaaS budget** in this package. Paid ticket ≠ willingness to buy planning software.

## Channel bias

| Bias | Effect on this package |
|---|---|
| Public marketing pages | Overstate polish; hide back-office pain and tool spend |
| Meetup/Partiful discovery | Over-represents social/stranger formats vs private friend groups with a paid host |
| Exa Reddit snippets | Over-represents one-off birthday/tourist logistics; under-represents professional hosts |
| Blocked Reddit/X login | Cannot validate comment quality, recency, or local NYC host subcultures deeply |
| English web / NYC search framing | Confirms NYC density we already assumed; circular reinforcement risk |
| No outbound interviews | Survivorship of *visible* operators only; quiet hosts invisible |

## Is the current workaround sufficient?

**For many friend groups: yes** — chat + DinePoll/Form + OpenTable is enough; they will not pay.

**For weekly paid micro-hosts rotating restaurants: maybe not** — they already run questionnaires and booking ops; a tool that shortens candidate checking + handles private veto/replan *could* matter — **unproven without talking to them**.

**For scaled platforms: workaround is their product** — they will not buy Common Ground; they are competitors.

## Falsifiers still required (unchanged thresholds)

- Live hosts saying venue rework is not their pain  
- Baseline poll/Form equally preferred to Qloo slate  
- Refusal to ask guests for any inputs
