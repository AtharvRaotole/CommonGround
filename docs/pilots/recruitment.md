## Path A — friend / colleague group (PRIMARY)

### Who to ask

Pick **one** upcoming outing (next 3–14 days) with 4–8 adults who already know each other. You are the host. Ideal: dinner in Manhattan south of 59th or Williamsburg corridor. If the outing is elsewhere, note the catchment override in results.

### Message (copy/paste)

Hey — I'm running a short planning experiment for a project (Common Ground / Qloo hackathon). For our [DAY/DATE] [dinner/brunch], I want to try a better way to pick the place.

If you're in:

1. Reply with up to 3 favorite artists / films / books / places (optional — you can skip).
2. Tell me any hard needs (budget max, vegetarian/vegan, neighborhood limits, accessibility, "no X cuisine").
3. Later I'll send 2 shortlists of restaurants **without saying how they were made**. Rate 1–5 how willing you'd be to go to the top pick of each. Takes ~5 minutes total.

Private tastes stay private (I won't share your list with the group). I'm not booking anything for you — we still decide and reserve ourselves.

Reply YES + your constraints if you're in. Skip is fine too.

## Path B — near-ICP stretch (parallel, not required for AC01)

Use only if you have 20 minutes for cold outreach. Priority: H02 → H01 → H16 → H03 → H05 → H09.

### Subject

Quick research ask — how you pick restaurants for small groups

### Body

Hi — I saw [specific public outing] that you organize. I’m building a planning tool for hosts of recurring small-group dinners and would value 15 minutes on how you currently choose venues and handle conflicting preferences.

Separately, if you have an upcoming outing in the next two weeks, I can prepare a free evidence-backed shortlist for that group (guests optionally share a few cultural interests + hard needs; you keep all booking control). Either conversation alone is useful.

I’m Atharv — independent builder, Qloo hackathon project Common Ground. Not affiliated with Meetup/Partiful.
