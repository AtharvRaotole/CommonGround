# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

White product field for Common Ground. Near-white `#F7F6F3`, ink `#121417`, moss `#1F6B4A`. Satoshi for display, body, and UI. Signature mark: two overlapping circles with a shared lens.

## Surfaces

`/demo` is the showcase: product intro plus a film window that plays Collect → Export. No dark stage rails. Landing shares the mark, moss CTAs, and headline voice.

## Motion

Premium personality: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`, 160 / 380 / 560ms. Focal moment is the film reel. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Refuse

Eyebrow kickers, purple gradients, nested cards, em dashes in product copy, presentation-wizard layouts as the whole page.
