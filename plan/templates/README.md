# Working templates

These are blank tools for future research and verification, not completed evidence. Duplicate them into the future product's private evidence directory. Keep personal records out of the public repository.

- [Verification record](verification-record.md): one AC, one observed result and its evidence.
- [Interview and outreach kit](interview-and-outreach.md): consent, behavior prompts, qualification and draft messages.
- [Experiment record](experiment-record.md): frozen hypothesis, comparable conditions, denominators and result.
- [Provider questions](provider-questions.md): concrete Qloo access/rights questions; draft only.
- [Decision record](decision-record.md): a reasoned continue/pivot/stop decision.

The [phase manifest](../phases.json) is the task tracker. Every item starts planned. Do not use checked boxes or example records as proof that work occurred.
